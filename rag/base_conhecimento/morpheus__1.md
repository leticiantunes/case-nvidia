NVIDIA Morpheus is a GPU-accelerated, end-to-end AI framework that enables developers to create optimized applications for filtering, processing, and classifying large volumes of streaming cybersecurity data. Morpheus incorporates AI to reduce the time and cost associated with identifying, capturing, and acting on threats, bringing a new level of security to the data center, cloud, and edge. Morpheus also extends human analysts' capabilities with generative AI by automating real-time analysis and responses, producing synthetic data to train AI models that identify risks accurately, and run what-if scenarios.

The framework supports multiple pathways for developers to begin working with the platform. Morpheus is available as open-source software on GitHub for those interested in using pre-release features and building from source. Additionally, the digital fingerprinting threat detection and spear phishing detection AI workflows include AI frameworks and resources such as Helm charts, Jupyter Notebooks, and documentation to provide developers a foundation when building AI solutions. For production deployments, NVIDIA AI Enterprise offers unlimited usage on all clouds, access to NVIDIA AI experts, and long-term support.

Spear phishing represents one of the largest and most costly cyber threats to organizations. Customized for individuals and typically very convincing, spear phishing emails are difficult to defend against due to limited training data. The Morpheus platform addresses this through specialized detection capabilities built around the framework.

As connected users and devices expand, enterprises generate more data than they can collect and analyze, creating cybersecurity challenges. Digital fingerprinting enables organizations to build a profile of every user on the network to better detect anomalies and identify insider threats more rapidly.
