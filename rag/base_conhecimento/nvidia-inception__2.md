The NVIDIA VC Alliance is a global network of AI investors who leverage NVIDIA's technical expertise and resources to enhance portfolio performance. The initiative brings together venture capital firms, corporate venture arms, accelerators, incubators, and family offices that actively invest in and support AI and technology startups. Members gain ecosystem insights for more informed investment decisions, curated deal flow with startup connections, and portfolio support designed to accelerate growth. The program is built as a flywheel connecting founders, technology leaders, and customers into an ecosystem where AI startups can innovate rapidly while gaining immediate access to leading capabilities.

Portfolio enablement through NVIDIA Inception: VC Alliance members can accelerate startup growth by routing portfolio companies into NVIDIA Inception, which provides free cloud credits, developer tools and training, and go-to-market support. Portfolio companies referred by an alliance member receive fast-tracked application review, unlocking access to the latest developer tools, training, preferred pricing on NVIDIA hardware and software, and complimentary cloud credits. Members can refer portfolio companies through forms in the newsletter or through the dedicated VC Alliance portal.

Curated deal flow and founder connections: VC Alliance members gain visibility into a network exceeding 30,000 high-growth AI startups. The program facilitates founder introductions and exclusive pitch events that streamline deal discovery and evaluation, allowing investors to identify opportunities more efficiently while building relationships across the expanding AI ecosystem.

Market insights and technical expertise: members access virtual tech talks and in-person executive briefings covering emerging industry trends, breakthrough technologies, and startup opportunities. This educational component helps investors understand the technical landscape and make better-informed allocation decisions in the AI sector. Additional benefits include closed-door gatherings where top investors and NVIDIA experts share insights, promotional opportunities across the NVIDIA ecosystem, and free or discounted AI training for investor teams.

Ecosystem initiatives: NVIDIA announced a Global NOVA Initiative committing GBP 2 billion to catalyze the United Kingdom's AI startup ecosystem and accelerate creation of new AI businesses, with partners including Accel, Air Street Capital, Balderton, Hoxton Ventures, and Phoenix Court joining the commitment. NVIDIA has also partnered with European venture firms including Accel, Elaia, Partech, and Sofinnova Partners to offer DGX Cloud Lepton marketplace credits to portfolio companies, enabling startups to access accelerated computing and scale regional development more efficiently.

Eligibility: the VC Alliance accepts institutional venture capital firms, corporate venture arms, accelerators, incubators, and family offices that actively invest in and support AI and technology startups. Applicants must maintain an active corporate website and demonstrate a track record of relevant portfolio investments while meeting additional eligibility and compliance criteria. The program excludes credit, debt, or structured finance investors such as private credit funds, lenders, and banks; consulting firms offering services to startups; firms focused solely on Web3, blockchain, or crypto-only investments; and entities based in or primarily operating from embargoed or restricted countries.
