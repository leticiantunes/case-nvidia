NVIDIA Inception is a complimentary program designed to support AI startups throughout their development lifecycle, guiding members from prototype through production and meeting companies wherever they are in their growth journey. Inception delivers benefits crafted to help organizations discover new AI opportunities, build products with cutting-edge technologies, and scale their business operations. The program has no application fees, no membership charges, no deadlines or cohorts, and takes no equity, making it accessible to startups at any funding stage and regardless of whether they already use NVIDIA technology.

Training and guidance: members gain access to free self-paced technical courses alongside discounted expert-led workshops. The program provides personalized recommendations for the best tools and resources for each company's specific challenges, unlimited access to the NVIDIA Developer Forums for resolving technical questions, and a monthly member newsletter highlighting product announcements and exclusive member-only offers.

Developer tools and preferred pricing: Inception members can leverage the latest SDKs, model libraries, and developer platforms to solve contemporary AI problems. The program grants preferred pricing on select NVIDIA hardware and software; NVIDIA does not sell directly to Inception members but provides contact information for authorized suppliers holding inventory, and the preferred pricing functions as a rebate deducted from standard supplier pricing rather than a separate sales channel. NVIDIA cannot guarantee availability of particular products or provide early access to unreleased hardware. Members also benefit from exclusive partner offers giving discounted access to complementary tools, services, and technical support, plus free cloud credits from NVIDIA and partner companies, furnishing flexible and scalable compute for development and deployment without upfront capital expenditure.

Investor and capital connection: eligible members gain exposure to NVIDIA's venture capital network through Inception Capital Connect, which operates selectively based on individual company eligibility. The program organizes curated networking events bringing together startup founders, prominent venture capital firms, and NVIDIA executives to facilitate meaningful relationships between funding-ready companies and investors. Applicants provide funding-status information so NVIDIA can assess company maturity and identify organizations suitable for investor introductions; revenue generation is not required, as the program accommodates companies from pre-revenue exploration through scaled operations.

Brand amplification and go-to-market: members receive official Inception badges for their websites and corporate materials, co-branded social media content, and customizable marketing assets suitable for conferences and promotional activities. The program facilitates access to NVIDIA's international networks through premium industry events and strategic business introductions. As engagement deepens, participating companies unlock additional go-to-market and business development opportunities aligned with their growth trajectory.

Eligibility: prospective members must employ at least one developer, maintain an active company website, be officially incorporated, and be less than ten years old measured from the incorporation date. Companies not yet formally registered are ineligible until incorporation is complete. The program explicitly excludes consulting firms and outsourced development companies, any enterprise associated with cryptocurrency, cloud service providers operating as infrastructure platforms, resellers and distributors that primarily commercialize third-party technologies, and public companies.
